# cg-gym: storyboard

The real-motion gymnast rebuilt without footage, to find where code-rendered human realism stops.
Sources: MPFB 2 human (CC0 base mesh, default rig, procedural LAYERED skin), MakeHuman CC0 eyes, Poly Haven gym_01 (lighting and background), Poly Haven wood (rail). Code: uneven-bars apparatus, leotard and hair as material regions, IK hands on the rail, θ(t) from energy conservation (fast through the bottom, slow over the handstand), arch / hollow body shape by phase.
Not available here: MakeHuman skin, hair and clothes asset packs (the mirror served ≈ 2 KB/s), so skin is procedural and hair is a painted region.
