# doc-card: storyboard (14 s, 100 BPM, beat 0.6 s)

> Probe case: a Chinese rich-text card (`input/richtext.png`, 1080×1440, 3:4). It has a title band, three numbered tips with
> sub-lines, a highlight, a quote box and hashtags. OCR (chi_sim+eng) recovered all 9 lines, with a few symbol errors
> (① → "@"). The text on screen is typed from the source, not from OCR.
> Approach: **typeset, then act**. The card is rebuilt as live text on a painted card, not shown as a plate. Each tip gets a
> beat: its line types in, and Clawd acts it out beside it. The quote lands last. The text stays where the card puts it,
> so the video can be read as the card (PROJECT.text = 'content').

Logline: a card with three rules for video; Clawd breaks each rule, then fixes it.
World: the card itself, centred at 1080×1440 world px, on a desk. Palette from the card: coral #ff6b4a, cream #fffaf2, highlight #ffe08a, quote blue #5b7cfa.
Motif: the coral title band; it wipes on at the start and closes the video.
Arc: curious → busy → pleased.

Shots:
  A  0.00–2.40  [transition in: brush wipe in coral]  the title band wipes on and the title types out; Clawd peeks in from the right · push in
     reads:  0.00–1.00  the coral band
             1.00–2.40  the title
  B  2.40–5.80  [transition: camera move — tilt down to tip 1]  tip 1 types; Clawd holds up ONE finger (one thing per shot) and a 1.5–4 s timer arc fills
     reads:  2.40–3.80  ① 每个镜头只讲一件事
             3.80–5.80  the timer fills to 4 s
  C  5.80–9.40  [transition: camera move — tilt down to tip 2]  tip 2 types, the highlighter sweeps; an explanatory text bubble appears over Clawd's head, Clawd pops it and acts the word instead (a big happy jump)
     reads:  5.80–7.20  ② 用动作代替文字, the highlight sweeps
             7.20–9.40  the bubble pops; the jump
  D  9.40–14.00  [transition: whip pan down]  tip 3 types; Clawd walks into a cut with no action (freezes, sweat), then into one with a wave (smooth); the quote box draws on, the quote types; pull back to the whole card; iris out
     reads:  9.40–11.00 ③ 切点前要有动作
             11.00–12.60 the quote "Show it, don't write it."
             12.60–14.00 the whole card
  [transition out: iris close]
