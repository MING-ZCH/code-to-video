# p1-media: storyboard ("The button", 6 s, P1 media test)

Logline: Clawd wants to know what the big red button does, but it is very much not a good button, so it finds out from a safe distance.
World: a dusk field, peach sky, sap-green grass, a low sun; one palette across both shots. The blast adds fire orange and smoke violet.
Motif: the button's red recurs as the fireball's core.
Arc: neutral → thinking → mischief → surprised → scared → starstruck.

Shots:
  A  0.00–2.50  [transition in: iris open]  the field, the button on a post · Clawd notices it (eyes first), thinks, trots over, presses it · surprised take · slow push-in
     reads:  0.00–0.45  the field and the button (iris opens on it)
             0.45–1.00  Clawd sees the button (look, thinking)
             1.00–1.75  the mischief face, then the trot to it
             1.75–2.10  the reach and the press (cap sinks, click)
             2.10–2.50  "uh-oh": the surprised take holds
  B  2.50–6.00  [transition: flash cut — the blast's light floods the frame at the end of A and drains away in B]  wide: Clawd small on the left, far horizon right · the explosion rises; the shockwave reaches Clawd and pushes it back; the cap glows; starstruck · static camera with shake on the wave
     reads:  2.50–3.10  the fireball on the horizon (only thing moving; Clawd watches it)
             3.10–3.70  the shockwave crosses the field and hits Clawd (scared, sweat)
             3.70–4.30  the mushroom cap forms (hold)
             4.30–5.30  Clawd goes starstruck, arms up
             5.30–6.00  iris closes on Clawd
  [transition out: iris close]

Sound (engine/audio.js), tied to the same constants: thuds on the trot, click + pop on the press, boom on BOOM (= the cut), rumble bed, whoosh on the wave, chime on the starstruck take; a pad per shot.
