# 瘫坐长椅原子弹爆炸 (the bench meme)
A figure sits slumped on a park bench, completely unbothered, while a nuclear mushroom cloud rises on the horizon behind it.
The joke is the contrast: the end of the world in the background, total indifference in the foreground.
Remix format: the same moment repeats in one art style after another, each style its own shader, with sound made by code.
The figure finally turns to the viewer, puts on sunglasses, and the picture closes.
