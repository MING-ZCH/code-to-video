# real-mobius: storyboard

Logline: a Möbius strip floats in a void; an ant walks round and round it, sped up; one fixed wide shot.
Correctness: the strip is generated from its definition (half twist, one surface, one edge; built as one closed solid so no seam appears); a centre line and two edge lines on the surface make the twist visible. The ant follows the centre line on the surface normal: after one turn it is on the opposite face of the same point, after two it is back where it started. The counter reads the same `laps` value the animation used.
Look: Cycles, Poly Haven studio_small_09 lighting with a black background (a void), glossy ceramic strip, glossy black ant; motion blur from the speed-up.
