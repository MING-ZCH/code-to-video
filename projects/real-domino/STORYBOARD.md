# real-domino: storyboard

Logline: wooden plaques stand in a heart on a table; a fingertip tips the first one and the heart topples round, turning from raw wood to red lacquer.
Physics: Bullet rigid bodies, baked; friction, restitution and sliding come from the solver. The outline is arcs and tangent lines with a rounded tip (radius 0.16 m), because a falling plaque cannot reach round a sharp point (a parametric heart stopped the chain at its tip in three attempts).
Look: Cycles; Poly Haven plywood (front faces), red lacquer with a gold ring pattern (backs), wood table, studio HDRI. Overhead camera, one continuous shot.
Stand-in: the hand is a fingertip capsule with a skin shader; no realistic hand model was available.
