# cg-launch: storyboard

The real-world launch, rebuilt without footage, to find where code-rendered realism stops.
Sources: NASA 3D Resources SLS model (printable STL, no textures: materials are assigned in code by part), Poly Haven rogland_clear_night (lighting only; the camera sees a black sky), Poly Haven concrete. Everything else is code: mobile launcher (deck, lattice tower, umbilical arms), xenon floodlights, exhaust cores (blackbody volumes) and steam clouds (4D-noise volumes that billow out of the trench), ascent z = (T/W − 1) g t² / 2 with T/W = 1.5.
Timing: core-stage engines at 0.54 s (frame 14), liftoff at 2.54 s (frame 62); the clock uses the same liftoff time.
