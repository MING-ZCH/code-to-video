# real-motion: storyboard

Logline: a gymnast's giant swings on the high bar, opened as a title sequence, pushed into slow motion at the moment her body lies flat above the bar, and closed by turning the real footage into an ink painting.
World: one real source clip (`highbar`, Gymnastics Australia 020, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0, LauraHale), warm film grade, 2:1 letterbox. Colour arc: dark and defocused (title) → full colour → ink on paper.
Motif: the title words. They open the film and return, small, at the end.
Source time: one curve for the whole film (`speedRamp`), so motion is continuous across the shots.
